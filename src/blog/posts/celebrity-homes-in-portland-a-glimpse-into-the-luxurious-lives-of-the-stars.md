**A Star-Studded Neighborhood: Dunthorpe**

*Our first stop on this tour of celebrity homes brings us to the exclusive enclave of Dunthorpe. Nestled along the Willamette River, this neighborhood is renowned for its privacy, natural beauty, and, of course, its celebrity residents.*

**The Courteney Cox Estate:**

*Known for her iconic role on the hit TV series "Friends," Courteney Cox chose Dunthorpe as her serene retreat. Her luxurious estate boasts stunning riverfront views, a private dock, and meticulously landscaped grounds. The Mediterranean-style villa exudes elegance and offers a tranquil escape from the bustling entertainment industry.*

**A Journey to the West Hills: Keanu Reeves' Hideaway**

*The West Hills, with its picturesque views and opulent estates, is another magnet for celebrities seeking respite from the spotlight. In this lush landscape, we find the home of the enigmatic Keanu Reeves.*

**Keanu Reeves' Sanctuary:**

*Known for his roles in "The Matrix" and "John Wick" series, Keanu Reeves has created a sanctuary in the West Hills. His modern, eco-friendly mansion seamlessly integrates with the natural surroundings. Floor-to-ceiling windows provide panoramic views of the city, while sustainable design elements reflect his commitment to a harmonious coexistence with nature.*

**Urban Luxury: The Pearl District's Attraction**

*For those celebrities who prefer the vibrant heart of the city, the Pearl District offers a blend of urban energy and luxury living.*

**Eddie Vedder's Urban Oasis:**

*The iconic frontman of Pearl Jam, Eddie Vedder, has made the Pearl District his home. His penthouse, perched atop a sleek high-rise, offers breathtaking views of the Willamette River and the city skyline. With its modern design and artistic flair, this penthouse is a testament to Portland's creative spirit.*

**A Stellar Legacy: Beverly Cleary's Former Home**

*Portland is not only home to Hollywood stars but also literary luminaries. The late Beverly Cleary, beloved author of the Ramona Quimby series, once resided in a charming Grant Park home.*

**Beverly Cleary's Literary Retreat:**

*This elegant home, with its classic architecture and well-tended gardens, was the inspiration for some of Cleary's most cherished stories. While it may not have the glitz and glamour of Hollywood, it holds a special place in the hearts of readers around the world.*
