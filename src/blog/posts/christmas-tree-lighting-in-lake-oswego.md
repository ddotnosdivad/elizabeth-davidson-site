It’s that magical time of year when Lake Oswego starts to sparkle, and nothing kicks off the holiday season quite like the annual **Lake Oswego Tree Lighting Festival**! Whether you’ve been here for years or you’re brand new to town, this festive tradition is one you won’t want to miss. Picture a holiday marketplace, show choirs, Santa, reindeer and the big moment when the Millennium tree lights up downtown. It’s the perfect way to get into the holiday mood! And this year there’s a bonus: the Reunion Farmers’ Market lands in the very same park the Saturday before Thanksgiving.

If you want more holiday light displays, see the [best Christmas tree lightings in and around Portland](/post/the-best-christmas-tree-lightings-in-and-around-portland-or-your-guide-to-festive-fun).

## Quick facts

- **Tree Lighting Festival:** Friday, November 27, 2026, 3:00 to 7:00 p.m.
- **Tree lighting ceremony:** 5:30 p.m.
- **Reunion Farmers’ Market:** Saturday, November 21, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- **Where (both):** Millennium Plaza Park, 200 1st Street, Lake Oswego, OR 97034
- **Hosts:** the City of Lake Oswego and the Lake Oswego Chamber of Commerce (tree lighting); City of Lake Oswego Parks & Recreation (Reunion market)

## The 62nd Annual Tree Lighting Festival

The City of Lake Oswego and the [**Lake Oswego Chamber of Commerce**](https://lakeoswegochamber.com/) present the Tree Lighting Festival, and 2026 marks the **62nd annual** celebration. It’s held on the Friday after Thanksgiving, and this year that’s **Friday, November 27, from 3:00 to 7:00 p.m.** at Millennium Plaza Park.

Here’s what the city’s event page lists for this year:

- A **holiday marketplace** featuring local food, artists, makers and holiday-themed vendors
- A **Local Winery Row**
- **Photos with Santa**
- **Timberview Farm reindeer**
- **Letters to Santa** with the Lake Oswego Police Department
- Holiday performances by the **Lake Oswego High School and Lakeridge High School show choirs**
- Festive music from the [**Millennium Concert Band**](https://lakeoswegoband.org/)
- The **Millennium Tree Lighting Ceremony at 5:30 p.m.**

### The Glow of the Christmas Tree

It’s a true sight to behold when the tree is finally lit, and it instantly fills the whole park with a warm, festive glow. Once the tree lights up at 5:30, you’ll find yourself surrounded by a twinkling display that’s perfect for photos and making memories. (Don’t forget your camera, or better yet, your smartphone!)

## The Reunion Farmers’ Market: Saturday, November 21

If you’re missing the Lake Oswego Farmers’ Market, mark your calendar for the **Reunion Farmers’ Market** on **Saturday, November 21, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.**, also at Millennium Plaza Park. The city describes it as fresh from local farms to your Thanksgiving table, with many of your favorite market vendors returning to share their fall harvest. Think pumpkins, sweet potatoes and mushrooms, plus a few new fall recipes to take home.

According to the city’s Fall 2026 Activity Guide, the day also includes:

- **Live music** from 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.
- **Local artisans**
- **SNAP matching** up to $20
- **Farm Fresh Kids** activities
- **Horse-drawn wagon rides** through downtown Lake Oswego, about 20 minutes each. Reservations are required for rides on the hour, and first-come, first-served tickets are sold onsite on the half hour. Tickets are $15 for adults and $10 for youth, through loparks.org or 503-675-2549.

It’s such a lovely way to stock up for Thanksgiving dinner, and then you can come right back to the same park six days later for the tree lighting!

## Why the Lake Oswego Tree Lighting Matters

One of the things I truly love about the **Tree Lighting Festival** is how it brings our community together. It’s not just about the lights or the tree (though they are *stunning*), but about the sense of togetherness and joy that fills the air. Whether you’re greeting neighbors, meeting new friends, or simply soaking in the festive atmosphere, there’s something about the evening that brings out the best in everyone.

## Tips for Enjoying the Tree Lighting

To make the most of your time at the festival, here are a few tips:

1. **Arrive early:** The tree lighting is at 5:30 p.m., and the festival opens at 3:00 p.m., so come early to browse the marketplace and find a good spot before the 5:30 p.m. lighting.

2. **Dress warmly:** It’s Oregon in late November, so it’s likely to be chilly! Layer up with a warm coat, gloves and a scarf to stay toasty.

3. **Bring a camera (or your phone):** You’ll definitely want to capture some magical moments, especially when the tree lights up.

4. **Make an evening of it downtown:** After the ceremony, head to one of the [local eateries](/post/lakeside-dining-delights-the-best-restaurants-in-lake-oswego-oregon) for a cozy dinner or dessert.
