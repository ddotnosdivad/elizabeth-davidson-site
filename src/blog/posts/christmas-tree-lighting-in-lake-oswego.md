It’s that magical time of year when Lake Oswego transforms into a winter wonderland, and nothing kicks off the holiday season quite like the annual **Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting**! Whether you’re a longtime resident or new to the area, this festive tradition is one that you won’t want to miss. Picture twinkling lights, hot cocoa, carolers, and a glowing community spirit—it’s the perfect way to get into the holiday mood!

So, let’s dive into why the Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting is *the* event to attend this season. 🎄✨

### A Community Tradition with Holiday Cheer

The Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting is one of the most beloved events of the season, and it’s a time when the whole town comes together to celebrate the magic of Christmas.

The City of Lake Oswego and the [**Lake Oswego Chamber of Commerce**](https://lakeoswegochamber.com/)[**(link is external)**](https://lakeoswegochamber.com/) invite you to ring in the holiday season with the annual Tree Lighting Festival! 2024 marks the 60th anniversary of this time-honored tradition.

Festivities take place at Millennium Plaza Park, with holiday music and caroling, festive vendors, letters to Santa, photos with Santa, hot food, sweet treats and more! The celebratory lighting of the Millennium tree takes place at 5:30 p.m.

### The Glow of the Christmas Tree

It’s a true sight to behold when the tree is finally lit, and it instantly fills the entire park with a warm, festive glow. After the big countdown, you’ll find yourself surrounded by a twinkling display that’s perfect for family photos and making memories. (Don’t forget your camera—or better yet, your smartphone!)

### Why the Lake Oswego Tree Lighting Matters

One of the things I truly love about the **Christmas Tree Lighting** is how it brings our community together. It’s not just about the lights or the tree (though they are *stunning*), but about the sense of togetherness and joy that fills the air. Whether you’re greeting neighbors, meeting new friends, or simply soaking in the festive atmosphere, there’s something about the event that brings out the best in everyone.

### Tips for Enjoying the Tree Lighting Event

To make the most of your time at the Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting, here are a few pro tips:

1.  **Arrive early**: The event can get crowded, so it’s a good idea to arrive a little early to get a good spot for the tree lighting.

2.  **Dress warmly**: Even though it’s a festive celebration, it’s Oregon, so it’s likely to be chilly! Layer up with a warm coat, gloves, and a scarf to stay toasty.

3.  **Bring a camera (or your phone)**: You’ll definitely want to capture some magical moments, especially when the tree lights up.

4.  **Check out local dining options**: After the ceremony, head to one of the local eateries for a cozy dinner or dessert. A lot of restaurants around the area get into the holiday spirit with seasonal menus and decorations.

### A Perfect Way to Start Your Holidays in Lake Oswego

If you’re new to the area or just visiting, the **Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting** is the perfect introduction to the heart and soul of this beautiful town. It’s a time when the community truly shines, and the joy and warmth of the season are felt by all. It’s also a wonderful opportunity to explore Lake Oswego’s charming downtown and experience the magic that makes this town so special.
