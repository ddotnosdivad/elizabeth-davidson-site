Whether you’re a longtime resident or new to the area, this festive tradition is one that you won’t want to miss. ### A Community Tradition with Holiday Cheer

The Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting is one of the most beloved events of the season, and it’s a time when the whole town comes together to celebrate the magic of Christmas.

The City of Lake Oswego and the [**Lake Oswego Chamber of Commerce**](https://lakeoswegochamber.com/) host the annual Tree Lighting Festival, a tradition that has been running for more than 60 years.

**When and where:** The festival is usually held the Friday after Thanksgiving, in the afternoon and early evening (in recent years roughly 3 to 7 p.m.), at Millennium Plaza Park, 200 First St, Lake Oswego, OR 97034. The lighting of the Millennium tree usually takes place around 5:30 p.m. Check the City of Lake Oswego Parks & Recreation page or the Chamber’s site for this year’s date and schedule.

**What you’ll usually find:**

-   Holiday music and caroling, including local choirs and high school show choirs

-   The tree lighting ceremony, followed by a performance from the [**Millennium Concert Band**](https://lakeoswegoband.org/)

-   Photos with Santa and letters to Santa

-   Reindeer viewing in some years

-   A holiday marketplace with local makers, hot food and sweet treats (the vendor list changes every year)

### The Glow of the Christmas Tree

It’s a true sight to behold when the tree is finally lit, and it instantly fills the entire park with a warm, festive glow. After the big countdown, you’ll find yourself surrounded by a twinkling display that’s perfect for photos and making memories. (Don’t forget your camera—or better yet, your smartphone!)

### Why the Lake Oswego Tree Lighting Matters

One of the things I truly love about the **Christmas Tree Lighting** is how it brings our community together. It’s not just about the lights or the tree (though they are *stunning*), but about the sense of togetherness and joy that fills the air. Whether you’re greeting neighbors, meeting new friends, or simply soaking in the festive atmosphere, there’s something about the event that brings out the best in everyone.

### Tips for Enjoying the Tree Lighting Event

To make the most of your time at the Lake Oswego Christmas Tree Lighting, here are a few pro tips:

1.  **Arrive early**: The event can get crowded, so it’s a good idea to arrive a little early to get a good spot for the tree lighting.

2.  **Dress warmly**: Even though it’s a festive celebration, it’s Oregon, so it’s likely to be chilly! Layer up with a warm coat, gloves, and a scarf to stay toasty.

3.  **Bring a camera (or your phone)**: You’ll definitely want to capture some magical moments, especially when the tree lights up.

4.  **Check out local dining options**: After the ceremony, head to one of the [local eateries](/post/lakeside-dining-delights-the-best-restaurants-in-lake-oswego-oregon) for a cozy dinner or dessert. A lot of restaurants around the area get into the holiday spirit with seasonal menus and decorations.
