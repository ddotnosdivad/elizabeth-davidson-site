Hello, my fellow Portland foodies and holiday enthusiasts! If you're anything like me, Thanksgiving is all about family and food! Whether you’re a Portland native, new to the area, or just in town visiting, the good news is that you don't have to be chained to the stove this Thanksgiving. Luckily, Portland’s culinary scene has got your back! So, if you're in the mood for turkey, stuffing, or something a little more... unconventional, here’s a roundup of great places to feast in Portland this Thanksgiving.

Holiday menus, hours and prices change every year, so for each restaurant below, check their site for this year’s Thanksgiving menu and reservations.

![thanksgiving in portland](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_42ec2bfa00334ee6a86139e198a121a5.webp)

### 1\. Jake's Grill

For a classic Portland Thanksgiving dinner, head to Jake’s Grill (611 SW 10th Ave, Portland, OR 97205), a classic American grill that’s been serving downtown Portland since 1994 from the historic Sentinel Hotel. Jake’s has offered both a Thanksgiving meal at the restaurant and a Thanksgiving dinner to take home, with all the traditional fixings. The cozy, historic ambiance makes it a great place to get into the holiday spirit without the hassle of doing dishes.

**Pro Tip:** Make reservations early, because this place fills up fast, especially when there’s turkey involved!

### 2\. Campana

Campana (901 NE Oneonta St, Portland, OR 97211) is an Italian restaurant in Northeast Portland’s Woodlawn neighborhood. Its warm, welcoming atmosphere sets the stage for an unforgettable meal, whether you're enjoying an intimate dinner or a special celebration. Known for house-made pasta and risotto alongside meat and fish entrées, Campana delivers a menu that’s both familiar and inventive. Call ahead to find out whether they’re open on the holiday.

### 3\. Screen Door

**Screen Door** brings its signature Southern comfort food to the table, and Thanksgiving is no exception. Known for its hearty, flavorful dishes (and its famous fried chicken) and warm, welcoming atmosphere, Screen Door has a Pearl District location (1131 NW Couch St, Portland, OR 97209) and an Eastside location (2337 E Burnside St, Portland, OR 97214). This year the Pearl District location is open on Thanksgiving Day, **Thursday, November 26, 2026**, with a turkey dinner special, while the Eastside location is closed that day. Screen Door also offers heat-and-serve Thanksgiving dinner boxes to pre-order, and they often sell out early.

**Pro Tip:** Save room for pie! Screen Door’s Thanksgiving pies can be pre-ordered along with the dinner boxes.

### 4\. Portland City Grill

This Thanksgiving, elevate your holiday dining experience with a stunning view of Portland’s skyline and the Willamette River at **Portland City Grill** (111 SW 5th Ave, 30th Floor, Portland, OR 97204). Located on the 30th floor of the US Bancorp Tower, known locally as “Big Pink,” this iconic spot has served a special Thanksgiving menu that combines classic holiday flavors with a touch of elegance. Whether you’re celebrating a big gathering or enjoying a more intimate dinner, the spectacular panoramic views of the city provide the perfect backdrop for a memorable Thanksgiving.

**Pro Tip:** Be sure to book early! The views are as sought-after as the food, especially on a holiday like Thanksgiving.

### 5\. The Ritz-Carlton

For a truly luxurious Thanksgiving experience, look no further than **The Ritz-Carlton, Portland** (900 SW Washington St, Portland, OR 97205). The hotel has hosted a Thanksgiving brunch in its ballroom, with a lavish spread of classic turkey and all the traditional sides, gourmet dishes, seasonal desserts and live music, plus activities for kids. The Ritz-Carlton’s impeccable service and attention to detail ensure a stress-free, memorable holiday with all the trimmings, minus the kitchen chaos.

**Pro Tip:** Make reservations early, as The Ritz-Carlton’s holiday events fill up quickly. And don’t forget to treat yourself to a signature cocktail or two from their expert bartenders!

### 6\. Bullard

**Bullard** (813 SW Alder St, Portland, in the Woodlark hotel) offers a mouthwatering twist on traditional comfort food. Known for its wood-fired meats and creative takes on classic dishes, Bullard is a celebration of bold flavors and seasonal ingredients, and the warm, rustic ambiance of the restaurant creates a festive and relaxed setting for a holiday meal. Call ahead to see what they’re serving for Thanksgiving.

**Pro Tip:** Don't miss their signature wood-fired dishes and seasonal cocktails!

So, whether you’re craving a classic turkey dinner or something more adventurous, Portland has you covered this Thanksgiving! And for those of you who prefer to avoid the kitchen madness entirely—well, there's no shame in letting someone else do the cooking for a change. From fine dining to casual comfort food, there’s no shortage of delicious places to enjoy a Thanksgiving feast.
