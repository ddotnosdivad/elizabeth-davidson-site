Hello, my fellow Portland foodies and holiday enthusiasts! If you're anything like me, Thanksgiving is all about family and food! Whether you’re a Portland native, new to the area, or just in town visiting, the good news is that you don't have to be chained to the stove this Thanksgiving. Luckily, Portland’s culinary scene has got your back! So, if you're in the mood for turkey, stuffing, or something a little more... unconventional, here’s a roundup of the best places to feast in Portland this Thanksgiving.

![thanksgiving in portland](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_42ec2bfa00334ee6a86139e198a121a5.webp)

### 1\. Jake's Grill

For a classic Portland Thanksgiving dinner, head to Jake’s Grill, a beloved Portland staple that’s been serving up fantastic meals since 1892. This year, Jake’s is offering a full Thanksgiving spread with all the traditional fixings—roast turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, and pumpkin pie that will make your grandma proud. The cozy, historic ambiance makes it the perfect place to get into the holiday spirit without the hassle of doing dishes.

**Pro Tip:** Make reservations early, because this place fills up fast, especially when there’s turkey involved!

### 2\. Campana

The restaurant’s modern, sleek design and warm, welcoming atmosphere set the stage for an unforgettable meal, whether you're enjoying an intimate dinner or a special celebration. Known for its exceptional pasta, wood-fired pizzas, and expertly crafted cocktails, Campana delivers a menu that’s both familiar and inventive. With a focus on seasonal ingredients and an ever-evolving wine list, every visit feels like a new culinary adventure.

### 3\. Campana

This Thanksgiving, **Screen Door** in Portland is bringing its signature Southern comfort food to the table with a festive twist. Known for its hearty, flavorful dishes and warm, welcoming atmosphere, Screen Door offers a Thanksgiving menu that’s both traditional and uniquely Southern. Think succulent roast turkey with gravy, cornbread stuffing, mashed potatoes, and their famous fried chicken—plus a variety of delicious sides like mac 'n' cheese, sweet potato casserole, and collard greens. If you’re craving something indulgent and comforting, this is the place to be. Screen Door’s Thanksgiving meal is perfect for those who want to enjoy a laid-back, flavorful holiday feast without the stress of cooking at home.

**Pro Tip:** Save room for their incredible pies—especially the buttermilk pie, which is a crowd favorite!

### 4\. Portland City Grill

This Thanksgiving, elevate your holiday dining experience with a stunning view of Portland’s skyline and the Willamette River at **Portland City Grill**. Located on the 30th floor of the US Bancorp Tower, this iconic spot serves up a special Thanksgiving menu that combines classic holiday flavors with a touch of elegance. Expect perfectly roasted turkey with all the traditional sides—think stuffing, mashed potatoes, cranberry sauce, and gravy—alongside signature dishes like cedar-planked salmon and rich seasonal soups. Whether you’re celebrating with family or enjoying a more intimate dinner, the spectacular panoramic views of the city provide the perfect backdrop for a memorable Thanksgiving.

**Pro Tip:** Be sure to book early! The views are as sought-after as the food, especially on a holiday like Thanksgiving.

### 5\. The Ritz Carlton

For a truly luxurious Thanksgiving experience, look no further than **The Ritz-Carlton, Portland**. This year, the renowned hotel offers a refined holiday dining experience, perfect for those seeking elegance and indulgence. Guests can enjoy a lavish Thanksgiving brunch or dinner in a beautifully appointed setting, featuring classic turkey with all the traditional sides, as well as gourmet dishes like roasted prime rib, lobster bisque, and decadent seasonal desserts. The Ritz-Carlton’s impeccable service and attention to detail ensure a stress-free, memorable holiday. Whether you’re celebrating with family or friends, this is the place to enjoy a sophisticated Thanksgiving meal with all the trimmings, minus the kitchen chaos.

**Pro Tip:** Make reservations early, as The Ritz-Carlton’s holiday events fill up quickly. And don’t forget to treat yourself to a signature cocktail or two from their expert bartenders!

### 6\. Bullard

This Thanksgiving, **Bullard** in Portland offers a unique and mouthwatering twist on the traditional holiday meal. Known for its wood-fired meats and creative takes on classic comfort food, Bullard’s Thanksgiving feast is a celebration of bold flavors and seasonal ingredients. Guests can enjoy a sumptuous spread featuring dishes like smoked turkey, cornbread stuffing, roasted root vegetables, and rich gravy, all cooked with the same care and attention to detail that Bullard is known for. The warm, rustic ambiance of the restaurant creates the perfect setting for a festive and relaxed holiday meal. Whether you're a Portland local or visiting for the season, Bullard’s Thanksgiving offerings are sure to make your celebration unforgettable.

**Pro Tip:** Don't miss their signature wood-fired dishes and seasonal cocktails—both are a perfect match for the Thanksgiving feast!

So, whether you’re craving a classic turkey dinner or something more adventurous, Portland has you covered this Thanksgiving! And for those of you who prefer to avoid the kitchen madness entirely—well, there's no shame in letting someone else do the cooking for a change. From fine dining to casual comfort food, there’s no shortage of delicious places to enjoy a Thanksgiving feast.
