Hey there, foodies and culinary adventurers! If you're on the hunt for a city where delightful dining meets laid-back fun, Lake Oswego, Oregon, is your gastronomic playground. As your trusty real estate sidekick, I've scoured the streets and sipped countless cups of coffee to bring you the lowdown on the best casual restaurants with kids' menus and easygoing atmospheres in the area. Buckle up your taste buds, because Lake Oswego is serving up a banquet of flavors for every age at the table!

![casual restaurants in lake oswego](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_b8caf7c65db142c9aa2012983437de6e.webp)

[**1\. Babica Hen Café:**](https://www.babicahencafe.com/)

*15964 Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

Brunch, anyone? Babica Hen Café is a local favorite, offering a cozy atmosphere and a menu that caters to both young and mature palates. From fluffy pancakes to hearty omelets, there's something for everyone in the brunch bunch.

[**2\. Pizzicato Lake Oswego:**](https://order.pizzicatopizza.com/order/pizzicato-lake-oswego-15180-bangy-rd)

*15180 SW Bangy Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

Pizza, pasta, and pure joy – that's the Pizzicato promise. This casual joint is perfect for pizza parties and pasta feasts. Plus, they offer gluten-free options for those with dietary adventures.

[**3\. Riccardo's Ristorante:**](https://www.riccardoslo.com/)

*16035 SW Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

Transport yourself to Italy without leaving Lake Oswego at Riccardo's Ristorante, run by Riccardo and Georgette Spaccarelli since 1980. This cozy spot serves up authentic Italian cuisine that will have your taste buds singing "That's Amore!" The warm ambiance, friendly staff and seasonal patio make it an ideal spot for celebrations.

[**4\. Gubanc's Pub:**](https://www.gubancspub.com/)

*16008 Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

Burgers, brews, and a side of fun – that's the Gubanc's Pub motto. This laid-back eatery, now back open in its remodeled space, is a local gem, with a menu that caters to all tastes. The kids' menu ensures the little ones leave with smiles as big as their appetites.

[**5\. Kyra's Bake Shop:**](https://www.kyrasbakeshop.com/)

*599 A Ave, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

Sweet tooth alert! Kyra's Bake Shop is a 100% gluten-free bakery that turns dessert into an art form. From cupcakes to cookies, everything is baked with love and crafted to perfection. It's a must-visit for anyone with a penchant for delectable delights.

In Lake Oswego, good food isn't just a meal; it's an experience shared with the ones you love. So, whether you're settling into your new home or just exploring the neighborhood, rest assured that Lake Oswego's culinary scene is here to turn every meal into a memory. Here's to happy tummies and even happier hearts in the city where friends and flavor collide!
