Ahoy, fellow flavor adventurers and chowder enthusiasts! If your taste buds are yearning for a seaside symphony of creamy goodness, you're in for a treat. As your friendly neighborhood real estate connoisseur and self-proclaimed clam chowder aficionado, I'm here to guide you through the best spots in Oregon to slurp, savor, and celebrate the glory of clam chowder. Buckle up your taste buds, because we're diving deep into the sea of savory sensations!

![Clam and seafood chowder in Oregon](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_5d9d0892bf054135bbc61e08e3de7dbc.webp)

## 1\. Mo's Restaurant - The Classic Cove of Chowder Dreams

*622 SW Bay Blvd, Newport, OR 97365*

First stop on our chowder trail? None other than the iconic Mo's Restaurant. Nestled on Newport's historic bayfront, Mo's has been a chowder beacon since 1946. Dive into a bowl of their legendary clam chowder, where the creamy goodness and tender clams will make your taste buds do a happy dance. Mo's Annex sits right across the street, and Mo's West is up the coast in Otter Rock.

## 2\. Local Ocean Seafoods - Fresh from the Ocean to Your Spoon

*213 SE Bay Blvd, Newport, OR 97365*

For a chowder experience that's as fresh as the sea breeze, head to Local Ocean Seafoods in Newport. Their commitment to sustainability and locally sourced ingredients elevates the chowder game. Every spoonful is a coastal symphony of flavors that transports you straight to the ocean's edge.

## 3\. Pacific City's Pelican Brewing Company - Sip, Savor, Chowder Repeat

*33180 Cape Kiwanda Dr, Pacific City, OR 97135*

Imagine enjoying your clam chowder with a side of breathtaking beach views. Pelican Brewing Company in Pacific City is where craft beer meets coastal cuisine, and their clam chowder is no exception. Pair it with one of their artisanal brews for the ultimate culinary adventure. Pelican also has brewpubs in Cannon Beach, Rockaway Beach, Tillamook, Lincoln City and Yachats.

## 4\. Chowder Bowl at Nye Beach - Where Chowder Dreams Come True

*728 NW Beach Dr, Newport, OR 97365*

Nye Beach in Newport is home to a gem appropriately named Chowder Bowl. Serving seafood and chowder since 1980, this spot is a haven for those who crave a classic. Order the clam chowder in a sourdough bread bowl and every visit becomes a chowder exploration.

## 5\. Fisherman's Market - A Fisherman's Tale of Chowder Triumph

*830 W 7th Ave, Eugene, OR 97402*

For a taste of chowder magic far from the docks, Fisherman's Market in Eugene is the place to be. Their clam chowder is a symphony of flavors, with a generous helping of clams and a broth that's nothing short of liquid gold. Bonus: Take some fresh seafood home from the market to recreate the magic in your kitchen!

## 6\. Clearwater Restaurant - Chowder with a Bayfront View

*325 SW Bay Blvd, Newport, OR 97365*

Clearwater Restaurant, situated on Newport's historic bayfront overlooking Yaquina Bay, offers a unique twist to the coastal chowder scene. Their clam chowder comes by the cup or bowl, with local pink shrimp on top if you like, and the windows look out on the bay's sea lions.

## 7\. Georgie's Beachside Grill - Chowder Chic with a View

*744 SW Elizabeth St, Newport, OR 97365*

Heading to the Central Oregon Coast? (More trip ideas are in the [Oregon getaways guide](/blog/city-guides/oregon-getaways).) Georgie's Beachside Grill in Newport is a chowder haven with a side of sophistication. With panoramic views of the Pacific, their clam chowder is a culinary masterpiece that combines elegance with comfort.

Who knows, your dream home might just be a chowder's throw away from one of these seaside sensations. Happy slurping, my friends!
