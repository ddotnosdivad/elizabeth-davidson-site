Ahoy, fellow flavor adventurers and chowder enthusiasts! If your taste buds are yearning for a seaside symphony of creamy goodness, you're in for a treat. As your friendly neighborhood real estate connoisseur and self-proclaimed clam chowder aficionado, I'm here to guide you through the best spots in Oregon to slurp, savor, and celebrate the glory of clam chowder. Buckle up your taste buds, because we're diving deep into the sea of savory sensations!

![Clam and seafood chowder in Oregon](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/11062b_5d9d0892bf054135bbc61e08e3de7dbc~mv2.jpg)

**1\. Mo's Restaurant - The Classic Cove of Chowder Dreams**

First stop on our chowder trail? None other than the iconic Mo's Restaurant. Nestled along the stunning Oregon coastline, Mo's has been a chowder beacon since 1940. Dive into a bowl of their legendary clam chowder, where the creamy goodness and tender clams will make your taste buds do a happy dance.

Newport & PDX Airport

**2\. Local Ocean Seafoods - Fresh from the Ocean to Your Spoon**

For a chowder experience that's as fresh as the sea breeze, head to Local Ocean Seafoods in Newport. Their commitment to sustainability and locally sourced ingredients elevates the chowder game. Every spoonful is a coastal symphony of flavors that transports you straight to the ocean's edge.

Newport, OR

**3\. Pacific City's Pelican Brewing Company - Sip, Savor, Chowder Repeat**

Imagine enjoying your clam chowder with a side of breathtaking beach views. Pelican Brewing Company in Pacific City is where craft beer meets coastal cuisine, and their clam chowder is no exception. Pair it with one of their artisanal brews for the ultimate culinary adventure.

Multiple Locations

**4\. Chowder Bowl at Nye Beach - Where Chowder Dreams Come True**

Nye Beach in Newport is home to a gem appropriately named Chowder Bowl. With a menu dedicated to the art of chowder crafting, this spot is a haven for those who crave variety. Classic clam chowder, seafood bisque, and creative daily specials ensure that every visit is a chowder exploration.

Newport, OR

**5\. Fisherman's Market - A Fisherman's Tale of Chowder Triumph**

For a taste of chowder magic crafted by seasoned fishermen, Fisherman's Market in Eugene is the place to be. Their clam chowder is a symphony of flavors, with a generous helping of clams and a broth that's nothing short of liquid gold. Bonus: Take some fresh seafood home to recreate the magic in your kitchen!

Eugene, OR

**6\. Clearwater Restaurant - Chowder with a Riverfront View**

Clearwater Restaurant, situated along the Rogue River in Grants Pass, offers a unique twist to the coastal chowder scene. Their clam chowder is a delightful surprise in the heart of Southern Oregon, proving that you don't need an ocean view to enjoy a bowl of seaside bliss.

Newport, OR

**7\. Georgie's Beachside Grill - Chowder Chic with a View**

Heading to the Central Oregon Coast? Georgie's Beachside Grill in Newport is a chowder haven with a side of sophistication. With panoramic views of the Pacific, their clam chowder is a culinary masterpiece that combines elegance with comfort.

Newport, OR

So, whether you're a seasoned chowder connoisseur or a first-time slurper, Oregon's clam chowder scene is a culinary adventure waiting to be explored. Who knows, your dream home might just be a chowder's throw away from one of these seaside sensations. Happy slurping, my friends! 🍲🌊
