Hello, brunch enthusiasts and potential Lake Oswego homeowners! As a dedicated realtor, I not only help you find the perfect home but also guide you through the delightful aspects of life in this charming city. Today, let's embark on a culinary adventure to discover the best brunch spots that make Lake Oswego a haven for foodies.

![best brunch in Lake Oswego](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/5e8760804ad34282ac257dba59c4f2a0.jpg)

**1\.** [**Babica Hen Café: A Cozy Culinary Retreat**](https://www.babicahencafe.com/)

Nestled in the heart of Lake Oswego, Babica Hen Café is a local favorite known for its warm atmosphere and delectable brunch offerings. From their famous eggs Benedict to mouthwatering sweet creations like the lemon ricotta pancakes, every dish is a testament to their commitment to quality ingredients and culinary craftsmanship.

**2\.** [**Nicoletta's Table and Marketplace: A Taste of Italy in Lake Oswego**](https://www.nicolettastable.com/)

For those craving a taste of Italy during brunch, look no further than Nicoletta's Table and Marketplace. This charming establishment combines the ambiance of a European market with the flavors of authentic Italian cuisine. Enjoy a leisurely brunch with dishes like the frittata del giorno or the classic eggs Florentine.

**3.** [**The Harvest Wine Bar: Brunch with a View**](https://www.theharvestwinebarlo.com/)

Situated near the lake, The Harvest Wine Bar offers not only a delightful brunch menu but also stunning views of the water. Indulge in their creative and flavorful brunch options, such as the smoked salmon scramble or the brioche French toast, while sipping on a refreshing mimosa. It's the perfect setting for a relaxing weekend brunch experience.

**4\.** [**Jefe: Southwest Flair for Brunch Lovers**](https://www.jefemex.com/about)

For a brunch with a Southwest flair, Jefe is the go-to spot in Lake Oswego. Their brunch menu showcases creative twists on classic dishes, such as the chorizo scramble and the chilaquiles. With a lively atmosphere and bold flavors, Jefe adds a dash of excitement to your weekend brunch plans.

**5.** [**Lola's: A bright spot on a cloudy day**](http://www.lolascafebar.com/)

Lola’s is a locally owned cafe bar nestled in the heart of beautiful downtown Lake Oswego. Formed from LO neighbor and restaurateur Joe Buck and nutritionist Katy Alstat, Lola’s is an inclusive and welcoming space for families, friends, colleagues, (and dogs) to gather.

**6\.** [**Tavern on Kruse**](https://www.tavernonkruse.com/)

A farm to table staple in a beautiful setting. Each season is a new celebration of flavors and textures specific and special to our region.

As you explore the real estate opportunities in Lake Oswego, take the time to savor the culinary delights that the city has to offer. From cozy cafes to upscale establishments, Lake Oswego's brunch scene reflects the city's commitment to quality living in every aspect. Join me in experiencing the brunch bliss that awaits in this picturesque corner of Oregon!
