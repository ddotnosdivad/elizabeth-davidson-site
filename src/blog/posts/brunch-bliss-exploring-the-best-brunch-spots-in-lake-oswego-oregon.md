**1\.** [**Babica Hen Café: A Cozy Culinary Retreat**](https://www.babicahencafe.com/)

*15964 Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

Nestled in the heart of Lake Oswego, Babica Hen Café is a local favorite known for its warm atmosphere and delectable brunch offerings. From their famous eggs Benedict to mouthwatering sweet creations like the lemon ricotta pancakes, every dish is a testament to their commitment to quality ingredients and culinary craftsmanship.

**2\.** [**Nicoletta's Table and Marketplace: A Taste of Italy in Lake Oswego**](https://www.nicolettastable.com/)

*333 S State St, Suite M, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

For those craving a taste of Italy for a late-morning meal, look no further than Nicoletta's Table and Marketplace. This charming establishment combines the ambiance of a European market with the flavors of authentic Italian cuisine. Their doors open at 11 for a leisurely lunch of house-made pastas and homemade pizzas.

**3\.** [**Jefe: Mexican Flair for Brunch Lovers**](https://www.jefemex.com/)

*16360 Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

For a brunch with Mexican flair, Jefe is the go-to spot in Lake Oswego. Their weekend brunch on Saturdays and Sundays puts creative twists on Mexican comfort food. With a lively atmosphere and bold flavors, Jefe adds a dash of excitement to your weekend brunch plans.

**4\.** [**Lola's: A bright spot on a cloudy day**](http://www.lolascafebar.com/)

*525 3rd St, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

Lola’s is a locally owned cafe bar nestled in the heart of beautiful downtown Lake Oswego, serving breakfast all day. Formed from LO neighbor and restaurateur Joe Buck and nutritionist Katy Alstat, Lola’s is an inclusive and welcoming space for friends, neighbors, colleagues (and dogs) to gather.

**5\.** [**Tavern on Kruse**](https://www.tavernonkruse.com/)

*4835 Meadows Rd, Suite 133, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

A farm to table staple in a beautiful setting in Kruse Village, with brunch on Saturdays and Sundays. Each season is a new celebration of flavors and textures specific and special to our region.
