Fall in Portland is pure magic — crisp mornings, fiery leaves, cozy sweaters, and the smell of pumpkin spice drifting from every corner café. As the days get shorter and the evenings cooler, it’s the perfect time to bring that warm, autumnal charm indoors. Whether you’re preparing your home for the holidays, getting ready to sell, or just love a good seasonal refresh, these DIY fall décor ideas are easy, affordable, and oh-so-Portland.

![fall home decorations](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_0d49e9844ae348fc83217d5d8742b292.webp)

#### 1\. Create a Pacific Northwest-Inspired Centerpiece

Bring the outdoors in with a rustic fall centerpiece. Gather pinecones from your neighborhood walk, add a few small pumpkins, and mix in greenery like eucalyptus or cedar branches. Arrange everything on a wooden tray or vintage platter for a look that’s both elegant and earthy — perfect for a Lake Oswego dining table or a cozy Portland bungalow kitchen.

![fall table setting
](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_56653f70f0b04145ba1afc868684863d.webp)

#### 2\. Layer Up Your Textiles

Think soft throws, chunky knit blankets, and plaid pillows. Swap out your lighter summer fabrics for rich textures in burnt orange, mustard yellow, and forest green. Layering textiles instantly adds warmth and comfort, making your home feel like the ultimate autumn retreat — even if the Portland rain has just started to fall outside.

#### 3\. DIY Pumpkin Projects (Beyond the Jack-o’-Lantern)

Skip the carving mess and try painting pumpkins instead! Use metallics for a modern touch or go classic with creamy whites and gold accents. Want something uniquely Portland? Try decoupaging your pumpkins with old maps of the city or pages from vintage books for a creative local twist.

#### 4\. Welcome Guests with a Handmade Wreath

You don’t need to spend a fortune at the store — a DIY wreath made from twigs, dried flowers, and fall leaves can be just as beautiful. Add touches of cinnamon sticks or dried oranges for a fragrant finish. Hang it on your front door and let your entryway greet guests with cozy seasonal vibes.

#### 5\. Add Ambient Autumn Lighting

As daylight fades earlier, embrace the glow! Mason jar candles, lanterns, and string lights can transform your space into a warm haven. Try placing a few along your front porch or in your backyard — perfect for sipping a glass of Oregon Pinot Noir under the twinkle lights.

![Porch and yard decorated for Halloween with jack-o'-lanterns and string lights](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_f602e3b2e5424f7f9ecf166afd478c25.webp)

#### 6\. Bring the Scents of Fall Indoors

Nothing says “fall” like the smell of cinnamon, clove, and vanilla. Create your own simmer pot with apples, orange slices, and spices on the stove — your home will smell better than any candle aisle in Portland!

### Fall in Love with Your Home All Over Again

Whether you’re settling in for the season or prepping to list your home, adding a few personal touches makes all the difference. DIY décor brings warmth, charm, and that special Portland personality into your space — and it doesn’t take much more than a free afternoon and a little creativity.

So grab your favorite flannel, put on a pot of coffee (or cider!), and let’s make this fall your coziest yet. 🍁
