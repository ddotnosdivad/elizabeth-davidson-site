Hey there, homeowner extraordinaire! 👋 Whether you’re staging your home for a big “For Sale” moment or just future-proofing your investment, one thing is crystal clear: **not all home improvements are created equal**. You don’t want to throw money into a project that gives you about as much ROI as a half-eaten sandwich at an open house (zero—trust me, I’ve seen it happen).

So let’s talk strategy. If you’re going to spend your hard-earned cash, **spend it smart**. Here's my list of home improvements that give you the biggest bang for your buck—and look good doing it, too!

![garage door replacement](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_5fcc14df163d4af68d395f4dcddf0aab_d_3872_2592_s_4_2.webp)

### 🔨 1. Garage Door Replacement

**Average ROI: 193.9%** Shocking, right? But a new garage door is like a crisp blazer—it ties the whole outfit (read: home exterior) together. It boosts curb appeal and adds a layer of security. Go for a modern, insulated model and thank me later.

### 🚿 2. Minor Kitchen Remodel

**Average ROI: 96.1%** We're not talking “open up every wall and install a Viking stove” here. Keep it simple: cabinet refacing, new countertops, updated fixtures, and maybe some fresh subway tile. Buyers LOVE a clean, modern kitchen—and they’ll pay for it.

![kitchen remodel](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_7b1afe7560a848eaa98ed0c4ebed4823.webp)

### 🪟 3. New Windows (Especially Energy-Efficient Ones)

**Average ROI: 65–75%** Buyers swoon for energy efficiency almost as much as they swoon for walk-in closets. New windows = lower energy bills, better insulation, and major resale appeal. Bonus points if they come with a lifetime warranty.

### 🚽 4. Bathroom Tune-Up

**Average ROI: 73.7%** Think of this as a bathroom glow-up: new vanity, fixtures, lighting, and a fresh coat of paint. Pro tip: swapping out that builder-grade mirror for a statement piece can change the whole vibe.

![bathroom remodel](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_2d8e665184934f8eac1ed3bfc63ccc1d.webp)

### 🌿 5. Landscaping + Curb Appeal Boosters

**Average ROI: 100%+ (yes, really)** First impressions matter, and a tidy, inviting yard is your home's handshake. Trim those hedges, plant some native greenery, add lighting—and maybe even a flagstone path if you're feeling fancy. ROI aside, it makes coming home feel *so* much better.

### 🧰 6. Attic Insulation

**Average ROI: 80–90%**Not glamorous, but very effective. Blown-in insulation can significantly improve energy efficiency, and buyers love knowing the home is as cozy as it is cute. It's one of those updates that shows you really cared.

### 💡 7. Lighting Upgrades

**ROI: Style points + 60–70%** Good lighting = good vibes. Swapping out old fixtures for modern, stylish ones can completely transform a space. Throw in some dimmers for drama and boom—you’ve got ambiance AND value.

### 🏠 Bonus: Fresh Paint (Inside AND Out)

**ROI: 100% feel-good, 55–60% ROI** Neutral tones = buyer bait. A fresh coat of paint makes everything feel clean, new, and ready to move into. It’s like giving your house a facial.
