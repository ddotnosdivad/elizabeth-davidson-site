If you've only ever zipped past Tigard on Pacific Highway, you've missed its heart. Tucked just off 99W, Main Street has been the center of town for more than a century, and in April 2023 it gained a new front porch: Universal Plaza, right on the Fanno Creek Trail. Let's take a stroll through Downtown Tigard and a few of the named neighborhoods just beyond it.

## Quick facts

- **Why Main Street:** the city says Main Street became Tigard's center after an electric commuter train line was built in 1910, when the town was known as Tigardville
- **Main Street America:** Tigard's Main Street is an accredited member
- **Universal Plaza:** opened April 15, 2023, at 9100 Burnham Street, along the Fanno Creek Trail
- **Homes:** about 555 homes in recorded subdivisions inside the city's Downtown neighborhood area (county records; older unplatted lots not counted)
- **Parking:** 263 on-street spaces and 33 spaces in public lots, according to the city
- **Rail:** the WES commuter rail station at Tigard Transit Center sits about 250 meters from Main Street

## How Main Street became the center of town

According to the city, the 1910 commuter line brought rapid growth around Main Street in Tigardville, with commercial buildings that housed restaurants, grocery stores, a dance hall, a blacksmith and a livery stable. If you'd like the backstory on the name itself, I wrote about it in [East Butte, Tigardville, Tigard: How Tigard Got Its Name](/post/tigard-name-origin).

Today the city lists the spring Art Walk, the Tigard Farmers Market and the Tigard Street Fair among the events on Main Street. The Street Fair is hosted by the Tigard Downtown Alliance.

## Universal Plaza

Universal Plaza is downtown's newest gathering spot, and I love how it ties Main Street to the creek. The city's park page lists:

- Two event lawns and the Jason Snider Community Tables
- A boardwalk and bike and pedestrian connection overlooking Fanno Creek
- A 4-stall restroom, drinking water, food carts and lighting throughout
- Tigard's first splash pad

The plaza and its featured sculpture were designed by RIOS, and the sculpture represents the four natural elements: water, air, earth and fire.

A heads-up on that splash pad: in its August 26, 2026 update, the city says it will remain closed for the rest of the 2026 season while required system updates and certification-related improvements are completed, and staff expect to present options for its future to City Council this fall. The Tigard Farmers Market made its home here on Sundays from May 3 to October 25 this year, 9:00 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. The city's listings give different hours for the plaza, so check its page before you go.

## Green space steps from the shops

One of my favorite things about Downtown Tigard is how quickly you can trade storefronts for trees. Fanno Creek Park stretches from Main Street to Hall Boulevard and the Tigard Public Library, and the city says it has more than 10 entrances, including one through the breezeway at the Tigard Civic Center.

Close by, the Tigard Heritage Trail is a three-quarter-mile path on a former railroad spur. It runs from Rotary Plaza on Main Street to Tiedeman Avenue.

## Getting around

Tigard Transit Center and its WES commuter rail station are about 250 meters from Main Street. TriMet's 12 Barbur/Sandy Blvd and 76 Hall/Greenburg run frequent service here, along with lines 94, 78 and 43. By car, the OR 217 on-ramp via SW Pacific Highway (exit 6) is about 1.7 road miles from the downtown area.

For parking, the city counts 263 on-street spaces and 33 spaces in public lots downtown. Two-hour limits on Main and Burnham streets now apply on Saturdays and until 7 p.m.

## What's next for downtown

Main Street and the downtown core sit in the City Center TIF District, which voters approved in 2006. It covers 228.96 acres, with a plan running to fiscal year 2035-36. The city, citing Metro, says 292 new multifamily units have been built downtown since 2006, and 31 downtown businesses have received building-improvement matching grants. With the district in the final years of its plan, the Tigard Town Center Development Agency launched a refresh of the 2009 downtown vision through a project called City Center Futures.

## Homes in and around downtown

In the city's Downtown neighborhood area, county records show house lots with a median build year of 1973. The 1960s (27%), 1970s (25%) and 1980s (20%) account for most of them, and the median lot is about 9,157 square feet. The area is in the Tigard-Tualatin School District: the district's 2025 attendance boundaries place most residential lots with James Templeton Elementary and a small share with Durham Elementary, and both feed Twality Middle and Tigard High. Always confirm a specific address with the [district's school locator](https://app.guidek12.com/tigardtualatinor/school_search/2025/).

Nearby, a handful of named subdivisions sit in neighboring city neighborhood areas:

- **Colony Creek Estates** (Durham Road area): about 128 homes on six plats recorded from 1979 to 1993, along streets such as SW Fanno Creek Dr and SW Colony Creek Ct. The Colony Creek Greenway runs right beside it.
- **Genesis** (Derry Dell area): about 96 homes on plats recorded in 1978 and 1979, on streets including SW Genesis Loop, Morgen Ct, Novare Pl and Eden Ct. The Pathfinder-Genesis Trail runs nearby.
- **Derry Dell**: the original Derry-Dell plats (1951 and 1955) hold about 41 homes on SW Derry Dell Ct, Watkins Ave and Park St, with a median lot of about 16,100 square feet. The name now covers the city's whole Derry Dell neighborhood area too.
- **London Square** (Greenburg Road area): about 38 homes on SW London Ct, built from 1983 to 1989, about 0.4 miles in a straight line from the Tigard Transit Center rail station.
- **Lady Apple** (Southview area): about 33 homes from a 2006 plat, built in 2007 and 2008, on streets including SW Jonagold Ter and SW Lady Apple Ln. The Scheckla Greenway is close by.
- **Clydesdale** (Greenburg Road area): about 24 homes from a 1973 plat on SW Clydesdale Pl, Clydesdale Ct and SW 106th Dr, with Woodard Park and Dirksen Nature Park both within about a tenth of a mile in a straight line.

## Tigard business of the week

After a walk along the creek, Main Street is a great place to refuel. A favorite is **Taqueria La Fuente**, an unfussy spot for regional Mexican dishes from family recipes, handmade tortillas and happy hour. You'll find it at 12198 SW Main St, Tigard, OR 97223.
