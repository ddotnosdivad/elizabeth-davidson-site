Here are some of the best restaurants in the [Willamette Valley](/blog/city-guides/willamette-valley):

## 1\. The Painted Lady Restaurant (Newberg)

*201 S College St, Newberg, OR 97132*

-   Nestled in a restored Victorian home, The Painted Lady is a haven for food aficionados. Owners Allen Routt and Jessica Bagley serve a seasonal chef's tasting menu that's nothing short of a work of art. Each dish is thoughtfully plated and showcases the flavors of the Willamette Valley in a way that will leave your taste buds in awe.

## 2\. The Joel Palmer House (Dayton)

*600 Ferry St, Dayton, OR 97114*

-   For those with a penchant for wild mushrooms and Pinot Noir, The Joel Palmer House is a must-visit. This historic restaurant, set in a charming old home, is renowned for its use of wild and foraged mushrooms, which the Czarneckis who run the kitchen have cooked with for over a century. Their seasonal tasting menu will transport you to a world of earthy, umami-rich delights.

## 3\. JORY Restaurant at The Allison Inn & Spa (Newberg)

*2525 Allison Ln, Newberg, OR 97132*

-   If you're a fan of farm-to-table dining with a touch of luxury, JORY Restaurant at The Allison Inn & Spa should be on your list. Their menu is a celebration of seasonal ingredients, and their extensive wine list perfectly complements the regional fare.

## 4\. Thistle Restaurant & Bar (McMinnville)

-   Thistle Restaurant & Bar takes pride in its dedication to sustainability and local sourcing. The result? A menu that bursts with freshness and flavor. The cozy, rustic setting adds to the charm of this Willamette Valley gem.

## 5\. SubTerra Kitchen & Cellar (Newberg)

-   SubTerra, formerly Subterra - A Wine Cellar Restaurant, is a hidden gem, quite literally. Located underground, this wine cellar restaurant offers a unique dining experience. Pairing their delectable dishes with a selection from their wine list is a journey for the senses.

Whether you're a resident of this beautiful region or a luxury real estate enthusiast exploring the area, these dining establishments offer a taste of the incredible flavors that make Willamette Valley truly extraordinary. Bon appétit!*
