The day after Thanksgiving has a sparkle all its own in downtown Portland. On Friday, November 27, Pioneer Courthouse Square hosts its 42nd annual Tree Lighting Ceremony, when Santa flips the switch on nearly 10,000 colorful lights. And if one evening of lights isn't enough, the Winter Wonderland drive-through light show at Portland International Raceway opens that very same night. Here's how to enjoy both.

## Quick facts

- **42nd annual Tree Lighting:** Friday, November 27, 2026, 5:30 to 6:45 p.m.
- **Where:** Pioneer Courthouse Square, 701 SW 6th Ave., Portland, OR 97204
- **What happens:** a community sing-along led by Thomas Lauderdale of Pink Martini, and Santa lights up the City's Tree
- **Winter Wonderland:** November 27 through December 31, 2026, at Portland International Raceway, 1940 N Victory Blvd.
- **Winter Wonderland tickets:** priced per vehicle or carload, not per person

## The 42nd annual Tree Lighting Ceremony

Pioneer Courthouse Square calls the Tree Lighting the highlight of the holiday season downtown, and I agree. The evening is a joyful community sing-along led by Portland's own Thomas Lauderdale of Pink Martini, joined by many of Portland's musical artists and chorale singers, and Santa Claus "flips the switch" to light up nearly 10,000 colorful lights on the City's Tree, which stays lit through the holiday season.

The program runs from 5:30 to 6:45 p.m. on Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving.

## Holiday performances on the Square

The fun keeps going after Friday night. The Square's events calendar lists holiday performances into December, including:

- **Saturday, November 28, 5 to 6 p.m.:** Get A Life Marching Band
- **Tuesday, December 1, noon to 1 p.m.:** Purple Moon Quartet
- **Wednesday, December 2, 5 to 6 p.m.:** Jennifer Holman

## A little history of "Portland's Living Room"

The block has quite a story. According to the Square's history page, the eight-story Portland Hotel, with 326 sleeping rooms, was built here between 1888 and 1890. In 1951 Meier & Frank bought the block, razed the hotel and built a two-level parking structure, and the Square notes that much of the hotel's original stone foundation remains under the sidewalks. A proposal for an 11-story parking garage in the late 1960s was denied by the City after heated public hearings, and Pioneer Courthouse Square finally opened on April 6, 1984, after an international design competition chose the Will Martin design.

While you're there for the tree, look for a few of the Square's art features: the bronze "Allow Me" umbrella man, the Weather Machine and the Mile Post Sign pointing to Portland's nine sister cities and other destinations around the world.

## Winter Wonderland at Portland International Raceway

Winter Wonderland is a drive-through holiday light show that opens the same night as the tree lighting, running November 27 through December 31, 2026, at Portland International Raceway, 1940 N Victory Blvd. Hours are 5 to 10 p.m. Monday through Thursday and 4:30 to 11 p.m. Friday through Sunday. From December 16 to 25, it's open 4:30 to 11 p.m. every night.

A few things worth knowing:

- **It's a fundraiser.** The show is the largest fundraiser for Sunshine Division, a food relief organization serving the Portland metro area since 1923, and its site says all proceeds go to its year-round hunger relief programs. PIR is the venue only; Sunshine Division runs the show.
- **Pricing is per car.** Standard vehicles are $38 online Monday through Thursday on non-peak nights, $49 online Friday and Saturday and $45 online on Sundays, and $50 online on peak nights, December 16-25. At the gate, those are $42, $53, $49 and $55.
- **Discount tickets.** Limited $35 discount tickets are available at Safeway and Albertsons customer service desks beginning the week of Thanksgiving, while supplies last.
- **Special nights.** Bike the Lights is Sunday, November 29, and the Lights & Leashes dog walk is November 30 and December 1. The site says no vehicles are permitted on the racetrack during the special nights of November 30 and December 1-2, so plan your drive-through for another evening.
- **Good to know:** chains and studded tires aren't allowed at the raceway, and on drive-through nights you stay in your vehicle for the whole show.

## Portland business of the week

A sweet treat downtown is a lovely way to round out a night of lights. A favorite is **Black Cat Frozen Custard & Coffee**, a speakeasy-style coffee bar serving Midwest-style frozen custard. You'll find it at 133 SW 2nd Ave #100, Portland, OR 97204.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the 2026 Pioneer Courthouse Square tree lighting?**
Friday, November 27, 2026, from 5:30 to 6:45 p.m. It's the 42nd annual Tree Lighting Ceremony.

**Who leads the tree lighting sing-along?**
Thomas Lauderdale of Pink Martini, with many of Portland's musical artists and chorale singers.

**When does Winter Wonderland at PIR open?**
November 27, 2026, the same night as the tree lighting, and it runs through December 31.

**How much is Winter Wonderland?**
Tickets are per vehicle, not per person. Standard vehicles are $38 online on non-peak Monday through Thursday nights, and peak nights (December 16-25) are $50 online.
