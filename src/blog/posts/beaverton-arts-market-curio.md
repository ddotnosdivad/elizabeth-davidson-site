If your holiday list has a few people on it who are impossible to shop for, I have a happy suggestion: skip the mall and head to The Curio in downtown Beaverton. The Reser and the City of Beaverton have filled the gallery at the Patricia Reser Center for the Arts with original work by nearly 40 regional artists, and this is an exhibition *and* a sale. It runs from November 6, 2026 through January 10, 2027, so there's plenty of time to browse, and come back again.

![Lit gallery windows showing paintings and a sign for The Curio exhibition at the Patricia Reser Center for the Arts in Beaverton, Oregon](/assets/img/blog/beaverton-arts-market-curio.webp)

## Quick facts

- **What:** The Curio | Exhibition & Sale, presented by The Reser and the City of Beaverton
- **When:** November 6, 2026 to January 10, 2027
- **Where:** Patricia Reser Center for the Arts, 12625 SW Crescent St, Beaverton, OR 97005
- **Building hours:** Wednesday through Saturday, noon to 6:00 p.m.
- **Special dates:** Shop Local Saturday on November 28 (noon to 6 p.m., free) and a First Friday Art Walk on December 4 (6 to 9 p.m., free)
- **Getting there:** steps from the Beaverton Central MAX stop, with the Beaverton Central District Garage right next door

## What you'll find at The Curio

The Reser describes The Curio as a marketplace of contemporary arts, crafts and small reproductions, and the range really is wide. Nearly 40 regional artists are taking part, with paintings, drawings, photographs, ceramics, mixed-media designs, glass, sculpture, fibers and wearable art. Alongside the original pieces, there's a selection of greeting cards, prints and small reproductions, which makes it easy to pick up something lovely at a smaller price point too.

What I like most is that you're not just buying a gift; you're meeting the work of artists from our own region and getting a feel for their individual styles. The Reser puts it nicely: buying unique works of art supports the creativity and livelihood of regional artists. That's my kind of holiday shopping.

![Patricia Reser Center for the Arts entrance with an "Art Gallery Open" sign on the sidewalk in Beaverton, Oregon](/assets/img/blog/beaverton-arts-market-curio-2.webp)

## Two dates to put on your calendar

The exhibition opened with an artist reception on November 6, and The Reser has two free events planned while it's up:

**Shop Local Saturday — Saturday, November 28, 2026, noon to 6 p.m.** The Reser bills this as its answer to Black Friday and Cyber Monday: a day to come in and support one (or many) of the nearly 40 artists in the show. It's free, and The Reser says it hopes to make it an annual tradition.

**First Friday Art Walk — Friday, December 4, 2026, 6 to 9 p.m.** An evening to wander the exhibition and discover new artists during the giving season. This one is free as well.

## When to go

The Reser's building hours are Wednesday through Saturday, from noon to 6:00 p.m., so plan your browsing for one of those afternoons (or for the Art Walk evening on December 4). The show closes January 10, 2027. If you have your heart set on a particular piece, I'd go earlier rather than later, since so many of these are one-of-a-kind works.

## Parking and getting there

The Reser sits at 12625 SW Crescent St in Beaverton Central, and getting there is easy:

- **MAX:** The Beaverton Central stop on the MAX Blue and Red lines is steps away.
- **Transit center:** The Beaverton Transit Center is about a quarter mile east, connected to Beaverton Central by the Crescent Connection multi-use path.
- **Parking:** The Beaverton Central District Garage, right next to The Reser, is open 24 hours. Its main entrance is on the west side of the building, off SW Rose Biggi Ave (3923 SW Rose Biggi Ave). The Reser lists the hourly rate at $1 per hour for up to 8 hours.
- **Drop-off:** There's a drop-off and pick-up zone on Crescent near the front entrance.
- **Bikes:** Bike racks are spread throughout The Round, next door, and around Beaverton Central.

## Beaverton business of the week

After an afternoon of art browsing, dinner just steps away is a lovely way to round it out. A favorite is **Mingo**, where Italian dishes get a refined Northwest twist at a trattoria with roll-up garage doors, a patio and a bar. You'll find it at 12600 SW Crescent St, Beaverton, OR 97005.
