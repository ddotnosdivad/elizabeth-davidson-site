Hello, Portland spirits and pumpkin enthusiasts! As your local real estate agent and Halloween aficionado, I’m here to dish out some spooktacular activities in our vibrant city each October. From haunted houses that will send chills down your spine to pumpkin patches bursting with autumn vibes, there’s no shortage of frightful fun. So grab your witch's hat and broomstick, and let’s dive into all the boo-tiful things to do for Halloween in [Portland](/blog/city-guides/portland)!

![Halloween in Portland](/assets/img/blog/wix/11062b_ca608181dd7f4c6dbdb4d8e71930dce8.webp)

### 1\. Haunted Houses Galore!

If you’re looking to unleash your inner thrill-seeker, the Portland area has some of the best haunted houses around. Check out **The Fear PDX** (12219 NE Glisan St, Portland, OR 97230), known for its immersive experience and spine-tingling scares. From creepy clowns to haunted mazes, this attraction is perfect for those who love a good fright. Its season usually opens in late September, and this year it runs through November 29, 2026. Up for a road trip? Head about an hour south to **The Nightmare Factory** in Salem (on the Oregon School for the Deaf campus, 999 Locust St NE, Salem, OR 97301), billed as Oregon’s longest-running haunted house, where you can explore themed rooms that will have you jumping out of your skin. Just remember to keep your screams to a minimum—your neighbors might be worried!

### 2\. Pumpkin Patches & Corn Mazes

What’s fall without a visit to a pumpkin patch? Head out to **Sauvie Island** for a day of pumpkin picking, hayrides, and navigating corn mazes. With stunning views of Mount Hood and an abundance of pumpkins, it’s the perfect spot for a day of [fall fun](/post/fall-fun-in-portland-family-adventures-for-kids-of-all-ages) or a cozy date. Grab some hot apple cider while you’re there, and don’t forget to snap a pic for the ‘gram—those fall colors are begging for it!

### 3\. Ghost Tours of Old Portland

For those who prefer their thrills with a side of history, consider joining a ghost tour. **Portland Ghosts** offers guided walking tours through some of the city’s most haunted locations, sharing eerie tales and spooky lore along the way, plus a haunted pub crawl. Most walking tours meet at the Harlow Hotel (722 NW Glisan St, Portland, OR 97209), and the pub crawl meets at the Benson Hotel (309 SW Broadway, Portland, OR 97205). Perfect for a spooky night out with friends, you might just walk away with a ghost story of your own!

### 4\. Creepy Crawly Craft Fairs

Get your Halloween spirit on at one of Portland’s many craft fairs! From the **Portland Flea** at The Redd (831 SE Salmon St, Portland), usually held the last Sunday of the month from April through October, to various artisan markets popping up around town, you’ll find everything from handmade Halloween decorations to unique gifts. Support local artists and bring home some one-of-a-kind spooky treasures to deck out your home!

### 5\. Portland’s Haunted Hotel

If you’re feeling brave (or just curious), why not spend a night in one of Portland’s most haunted hotels? **Hotel deLuxe** (729 SW 15th Ave, Portland, OR 97205) is known for its ghostly guests and old Hollywood charm. Even if you’re not staying the night, consider dining at **Gracie's**, where the ambiance is as cozy as the stories are spooky. Who knows—you might just encounter a ghostly apparition over your meal!

### 6\. Scary Movie Screenings

As Halloween approaches, many local theaters host frightful film screenings. Head to **Cinema 21** (616 NW 21st Ave, Portland, OR 97209) or **The Hollywood Theatre** (4122 NE Sandy Blvd, Portland, OR 97212) for special screenings of classic horror films. Cozy up with some popcorn and let those jump scares fill the room. Don’t forget your friends—watching a scary movie is always better when you have someone to cling to!

### 7\. Trick-or-Treating in the Neighborhoods

Of course, Halloween wouldn’t be complete without a good ol’ trick-or-treating adventure! Many neighborhoods in Portland, like **Alameda** and **Laurelhurst**, go all out with decorations and friendly neighbors ready to hand out candy. Grab your little ghosts and goblins and explore the decorated houses—it’s the perfect way to soak in the festive spirit!

So there you have it, fellow Halloween enthusiasts! Portland is bursting with fun and frightful activities every October. Whether you’re in the mood for a heart-pounding haunted house or a cozy pumpkin patch adventure, this city has something for everyone. And if you find yourself falling in love with Portland (it’s hard not to!), remember that I’m here to help you find your dream home among all the fall foliage and spooky charm.

Happy Haunting, and enjoy all the treats (and tricks) this Halloween season!
