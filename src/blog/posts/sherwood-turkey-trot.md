Before the turkey goes in the oven, a whole lot of Sherwood laces up. The Give n' Gobble Turkey Trot turns Thanksgiving morning into a 5K walk, a 5K run and a 10K run, and every bag of food you bring helps stock a local food bank. This year is the 20th annual, and I can't think of a better way to start the holiday.

![Elizabeth Davidson and her daughter in black caps and Give n' Gobble race bibs beside a running track at the 2025 Give n' Gobble Turkey Trot in Sherwood, Oregon](/assets/img/blog/sherwood-turkey-trot.webp)

That's my daughter and me at last year's Give n' Gobble, all smiles (and yes, that's a festive tutu). It's one of my favorite traditions of the season.

## Quick facts

- **When:** Thanksgiving morning, Thursday, November 26, 2026
- **Start times:** 5K walk at 9:00 a.m.; 5K and 10K runs at 9:05 a.m.
- **Where:** start and finish at Sherwood Middle School, 16956 SW Meinecke Rd, Sherwood
- **Events:** 5K walk, 5K run, 10K run, plus virtual 5K run, 10K run and 5K walk options
- **Registration closes:** Wednesday, November 25, 2026 at 12:00 p.m.
- **Food drive:** bring a bag of nonperishable food (or 3 cans or a box of cereal) to receive your shirt
- **Who it helps:** Helping Hands Food Bank, which is affiliated with the Oregon Food Bank

## Twenty years of Thanksgiving mornings

The Give n' Gobble started in 2007, when runner Cindy Aggson launched the first one with friends and family. According to the event's story page, she hoped for at least 100 people, and over 500 runners and walkers showed up. It has been a Thanksgiving tradition in Sherwood ever since.

Since 2023, the event has been coordinated in partnership with the Sherwood YMCA. The proceeds and food donations go to Helping Hands Food Bank, and the event's story page says those funds cover Helping Hands' annual expenses and make its backpack meal program possible.

## Picking the right distance

There are three in-person options. The 5K walk heads out first at 9:00 a.m., and the 5K and 10K runs start together at 9:05 a.m. The organizers ask everyone to line up by 8:45 a.m., with faster runners toward the front and walkers toward the back.

One thing to know before you sign up: the 10K is a run only. Because it's a holiday and the volunteers want to get home to their own Thanksgiving dinners, all participants need to be back by 11 a.m.

If you're out of town for the holiday, or you just want to join from your own neighborhood, the virtual 5K run, 10K run and 5K walk let you take part from anywhere.

## Prices and registration

On the registration page, the race fee for the in-person 5K run and 5K walk is $43 and for the 10K run is $50. The virtual 5K run and 5K walk are $29 each, and the virtual 10K is $35. Registration ends at noon on Wednesday, November 25, so don't wait until Thanksgiving morning to sign up.

The event is chip timed, with a disposable chip on the front of your bib, and there's no extra charge for timing.

## Bib pickup and the food drive

There are two ways to get your bib:

1. **Saturday, November 21 (recommended):** noon to 2 p.m. at the Sherwood Regional Family YMCA, 23000 SW Pacific Highway. You need to be registered by the end of the day on Friday, November 20, to pick up on Saturday.
2. **Race morning:** starting at 8 a.m. at Sherwood Middle School. Arrive early if you choose this option.

At either pickup, bring a bag of nonperishable food (or 3 cans or a box of cereal, or more!) to receive your shirt. If you're picking up for a family or team, all the bibs come in one packet, so one person can grab it and hand them out.

## Race-day tips

- **Parking:** park at Sherwood Middle School near the track, with backup parking in front of the school. The organizers ask everyone to use those two lots so the surrounding homes still have parking for their own holiday guests.
- **Rain or shine:** it goes on no matter the weather, so dress in layers.
- **Leave the bag in the car:** there's no bag check, so lock extra items in your car.
- **No dogs:** pets can't take part because of Sherwood Middle School and city policies.
- **Wheels stay home:** rollerblades, Heelies, roller skates, scooters and bicycles aren't allowed.
- **Water and snacks:** there are water stops on the course, and sponsors provide water, bananas, muffins and other snacks at no charge.
- **No awards ceremony:** the organizers put that money into chip timing and other upgrades instead, so you can head straight home to the kitchen.

## Want to volunteer?

The Give n' Gobble is volunteer powered. The organizers say about 150 volunteers help each year with registration, food collection and sorting, and course setup and management, and each volunteer receives a free shirt. You can sign up to volunteer through the event website.

## Sherwood business of the week

Once the race is run and the leftovers are put away, the long weekend is a nice time to explore something local. A favorite is **Anderson's Tavern**, a cozy tavern serving made-from-scratch Southern comfort food, with Sunday brunch. You'll find it at 21025 SW Pacific Hwy Spc A, Sherwood, OR 97140.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the Sherwood Turkey Trot this year?**
The 20th annual Give n' Gobble is on Thanksgiving morning, Thursday, November 26, 2026. The 5K walk starts at 9:00 a.m., and the 5K and 10K runs start at 9:05 a.m. at Sherwood Middle School.

**How do I get a Give n' Gobble shirt?**
Bring a bag of nonperishable food (or 3 cans or a box of cereal) to bib pickup on Saturday, November 21, or on race morning. Free shirts go to registered participants who donate, while supplies and sizes last.

**Can I bring my dog to the Give n' Gobble?**
No. Because of Sherwood Middle School and city policies, dogs and other pets can't take part.

**Where do the donations go?**
Proceeds and food donations benefit Helping Hands Food Bank, which is affiliated with the Oregon Food Bank.
