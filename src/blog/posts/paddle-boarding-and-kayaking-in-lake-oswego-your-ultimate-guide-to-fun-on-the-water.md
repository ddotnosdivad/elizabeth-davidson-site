### 1\. George Rogers Park

First up on our paddle paradise tour is the iconic George Rogers Park (611 S State St, Lake Oswego, OR 97034). This park is a local favorite and for a good reason. With its easy access to the Willamette River, you can glide along the serene waters while soaking in the lush greenery that surrounds you. It's the perfect spot to start your paddle boarding or kayaking adventure. And hey, after your paddling session, you can relax on the park's sandy beach or have a picnic with friends. A win-win, right?

### 2\. Foothills Park

Foothills Park (199 Foothills Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97034) offers more than just stunning views of the Willamette River. It's a fantastic launch point for your paddling adventures. The park's spacious dock makes it easy to get your board or kayak into the water without any hassle. Plus, the calm waters near the dock are perfect for practicing your paddling skills. Once you're feeling confident, you can explore the wider river and enjoy the breathtaking scenery.

### 3\. Millennium Plaza Park

For those who enjoy a lively atmosphere, Millennium Plaza Park (200 First St, Lake Oswego, OR 97034) is the place to be. Located in the heart of Lake Oswego, this park offers easy access to the lake and is surrounded by charming cafes and boutiques. Paddle out into the lake, and when you return, you can treat yourself to a delicious meal or a refreshing drink. It's the perfect blend of adventure and leisure.

### 4\. Lake Oswego's Main Lake

Of course, we can't forget the main star of the show – Lake Oswego itself. The main lake area is ideal for paddlers of all skill levels. The calm, clear waters provide a picturesque backdrop for your paddle boarding or kayaking experience. Paddle around the lake's numerous coves and inlets, and you'll discover why so many locals and visitors alike fall in love with this place. Don't be surprised if you find yourself paddling for hours, captivated by the lake's beauty.

### 5\. Tryon Creek State Natural Area

While not on the water itself, Tryon Creek State Natural Area (11321 SW Terwilliger Blvd, Portland, OR 97219) deserves a mention for its proximity and natural beauty. This spot is perfect for those who love to combine paddling with a bit of hiking. Start your day with a paddle on the nearby Willamette, then explore the park's numerous trails. It's a fantastic way to immerse yourself in the natural splendor of Lake Oswego.

### Tips for a Great Paddling Experience

-   **Safety First:** Always wear a life jacket and check the weather before heading out.

-   **Stay Hydrated:** Bring plenty of water, especially on hot days.

-   **Respect Nature:** Keep the waterways clean and be mindful of wildlife.

-   **Have Fun:** Paddle boarding and kayaking are all about enjoying the water and the great outdoors.
