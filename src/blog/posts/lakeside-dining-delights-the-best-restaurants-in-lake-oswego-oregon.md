Here are some of the Best Restaurants in [Lake Oswego](/blog/city-guides/lake-oswego) (for weekend mornings, see the [best brunch spots](/post/brunch-bliss-exploring-the-best-brunch-spots-in-lake-oswego-oregon)):

## 1\. Tucci Ristorante: Italian Elegance Downtown

*220 A Ave, Suite 108, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

-   Tucci Ristorante is where Italian culinary tradition meets downtown Lake Oswego. With a menu of homemade pasta, Italian favorites, and an extensive wine list, Tucci's transports you straight to the heart of Italy. The patio is perfect for a romantic sunset dinner.

## 2\. Gubanc's Pub: A Cozy Neighborhood Hideaway

*16008 Boones Ferry Rd, Lake Oswego, OR 97035*

-   If you're looking for a touch of warmth and hospitality, Gubanc's Pub is the place to be. This charming neighborhood pub, freshly back open in its remodeled space, offers hearty comfort food and a great selection of beers. Their Shepherd's Pie is a crowd favorite.

## 3\. Five Spice Seafood + Wine Bar: Pacific Rim Flavors

*315 1st St, Suite 201, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

-   Five Spice is where seafood with Pacific Rim inspiration takes center stage, paired with a Northwest-focused wine list. Don't miss their creative cocktails and the lake views from the deck.

## 4\. Nicoletta's Table and Marketplace: Italian Trattoria Perfection

*333 S State St, Suite M, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

-   Nicoletta's Table is a true Italian trattoria, serving up rustic and soul-warming dishes. Their homemade pizzas, house-made pastas, and farm-fresh ingredients will transport you to the Italian countryside. The ambiance is cozy and inviting.

## 5\. Baird's on B: A Hidden Gem Above Second Street

*485 2nd St, Lake Oswego, OR 97034*

-   Baird's on B is a true hidden gem, tucked upstairs at the corner of 2nd Street and B Avenue in downtown Lake Oswego. This American bistro offers a menu filled with Pacific Northwest flavors, seasonal ingredients, and a good selection of wines and beers. The covered outdoor terrace is perfect for open-air dining.
