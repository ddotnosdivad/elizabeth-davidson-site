The first week of December is one of my favorite times to be in Old Town Sherwood. Two community events land just four days apart: the Old Town Art Walk turns into a holiday market on Tuesday, December 1, and the Sherwood Holiday Festival brings a parade and tree lighting to Cannery Square on Saturday, December 5. Here's what to know so you can plan for both.

## Quick facts

- **Art Walk Holiday Market:** Tuesday, December 1, 2026, 5 to 8 p.m., across the six-block area of Old Town
- **Art Walk maps:** at Sherwood Center for the Arts, 22689 SW Pine St, or at any numbered sandwich board in Old Town
- **Holiday Festival parade:** Saturday, December 5, 2026, starting at 4 p.m. near 12th Street and Sherwood Blvd
- **Tree lighting:** after the parade at Cannery Square Park, typically around 5 to 5:15 p.m.
- **Cost:** the festival's hot dogs, cookies, hot chocolate and coffee are free while supplies last
- **Organizers:** Sherwood Center for the Arts (Art Walk) and the Sherwood Robin Hood Festival Association (Holiday Festival)

## Tuesday, December 1: the Art Walk Holiday Market

The Old Town Sherwood Art Walk happens twice a year, and the winter edition is a holiday market. Sherwood Center for the Arts pairs local artists, vendors and performers with the small businesses in the six-block area of downtown Sherwood, so each stop is a little different. You might view local art, chat with an artist, try an interactive activity, catch a performance or enjoy food and drinks.

Here's how it works. Pick up an Art Walk map at Sherwood Center for the Arts, or anywhere you see a numbered sandwich board in Old Town. Each business you stop in and spend a moment with stamps your map. Once you have a stamp from every stop on the route, turn your map in at the Center for the Arts to be entered to win a prize basket. With vendors joining the artists and performers, it has a real holiday-market feel.

The Center for the Arts itself is worth a peek, too. It broke ground on January 10, 2014, and its grand opening on February 28, 2015, drew more than 2,000 people.

## Saturday, December 5: the Sherwood Holiday Festival

The Sherwood Robin Hood Festival Association calls its Holiday Festival a beloved annual community celebration, and it fills an afternoon in downtown Sherwood.

**The parade.** The afternoon opens at 4 p.m. with a community parade of holiday floats, beginning near 12th Street and Sherwood Blvd and ending at Cannery Square Park in Old Town. Look for the Sherwood High School Marching Band, the Maid Marian Court and, of course, Santa Claus. If you'd like to be in it, the association invites entries on its website.

**The tree lighting.** Following the parade, everyone heads to Cannery Square Park for the Tree Lighting Ceremony, which typically happens around 5 to 5:15 p.m. Several local school choirs sing, Santa stops by, and there's a special sing-along with the Junior and Senior Maid Marian Court.

**Around Cannery Square.** There's a Santa Tent for sharing holiday wishes with Santa and meeting his elves, with free Santa surprise bags while supplies last, thanks to donations from local businesses. The Boy Scouts help serve free hot dogs and cookies, and there's complimentary hot chocolate and coffee, all while supplies last. The association also has a booth with Festival shirts and information about the all-volunteer nonprofit.

## A little history at Cannery Square

The tree lighting's home has a story of its own. According to the city's 2003 Downtown Streetscapes Master Plan, a brick plant operated on the cannery site in the early 1890s, the Graves Cannery was built on the old brickyard in 1918 to process fruit, and it closed in 1971. That area is now the Cannery Square part of Old Town. Today Cannery Square sits at 22622 SW Pine Street, right next to City Hall.

## Parking and getting there

The festival page doesn't list parking details. For events at Cannery Square, the city has pointed people to street parking and the Sherwood Center for the Arts parking lot, and there's a TriMet bus stop on Railroad Street one block away.

## Sherwood business of the week

After the tree lighting, a warm spot on Pacific Highway is a nice way to wind down. A favorite is **BearKat Brewing Company**, which runs a 7-barrel brewhouse pouring artisan-style ales and lagers. You'll find it at 20345 SW Pacific Hwy #404, Sherwood, OR 97140.

## Frequently asked questions

**When is the 2026 Sherwood Holiday Festival?**
Saturday, December 5, 2026. The parade starts at 4 p.m., and the tree lighting at Cannery Square Park typically happens around 5 to 5:15 p.m.

**Where does the Sherwood holiday parade go?**
It starts near 12th Street and Sherwood Blvd and winds its way to Cannery Square Park in Old Town.

**When is the Old Town Sherwood Art Walk Holiday Market?**
Tuesday, December 1, 2026, from 5 to 8 p.m. Pick up a map at Sherwood Center for the Arts or at any numbered sandwich board in Old Town.

**Is the Holiday Festival free?**
The organizer lists no admission, and the hot dogs, cookies, hot chocolate, coffee and Santa surprise bags are free while supplies last.
