Let’s talk about one of the most exciting (and nerve-wracking!) parts of real estate: **negotiation.** Whether you're [buying your very first home](/post/welcome-to-the-adventure-of-buying-your-first-home) or selling the one you've loved for years, one thing holds true—**you want the best possible deal.** And after 14 years in this industry, that’s exactly what I know how to deliver.

Here’s how I advocate fiercely for my clients—and why having an experienced negotiator in your corner makes all the difference:

![Smiling person working at a laptop on a white kitchen island](/assets/img/blog/wix/e2dd7e_c67522edbc074c0990a51e39ed372381.webp)

### 🔑 For My Buyers: I Fight for Value & Advantage

Buying a home isn’t just about falling in love with a space—it’s about getting that space at the right price, with the right terms. That’s where I come in. Here's what I bring to the table:

-   **Market Savvy:** I analyze comparable sales, current trends, and property history to make sure we’re submitting a *strong* but *strategic* offer.

-   **Winning Terms:** It’s not always about price, and [how your loan is structured](/post/demystifying-mortgages-and-financing-your-guide-to-home-loans) can matter too. I know how to structure offers that appeal to sellers—without compromising your needs.

-   **Tough but Fair Negotiation:** I ask the hard questions, challenge inflated pricing, and push for concessions that truly benefit *you*—from inspection repairs to closing cost credits.

### 💰 For My Sellers: I Maximize Every Penny

If you're selling your home, you deserve top dollar—and I make it my mission to get you there. Here's how I protect your equity:

-   **Price Positioning:** Using detailed market data, I help you price competitively (see how I [prep a home for top value](/post/summer-selling-tips-for-maximum-value-in-the-greater-portland-area)) to drive interest and *multiple offers.*

-   **Offer Analysis:** Not all offers are created equal. I break down every detail—financing, contingencies, timelines—so we choose the one that serves you best.

-   **Masterful Counteroffers:** I don’t settle. I negotiate smarter terms, higher prices, and cleaner closings, all while keeping your stress low and your goals front and center.

### 📈 14 Years of Real-World Results

The truth is: experience matters. Over the years, I’ve seen it all—bidding wars, lowball offers, tough markets, and fast-moving sales. I’ve learned when to push, when to pivot, and how to keep my clients in a strong position every step of the way.

And the best part? My clients know I’ve *got their back.* Whether it's your first move or your fifth, you can trust that I'm negotiating every detail like it’s my own home on the line.
